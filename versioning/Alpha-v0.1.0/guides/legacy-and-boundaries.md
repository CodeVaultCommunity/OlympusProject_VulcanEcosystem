# Other architectures and boundaries

[Português](legacy-and-boundaries.pt-br.md)

An app may retain procedural handlers, a framework or another internal architecture. Declare its actual public operations, channel contracts, configuration and assessed/unassessed internals. Participation in Vulcan Ecosystem does not grant Vulcan Architecture conformance.

The [legacy fixture](../tests/fixtures/legacy-ecosystem/README.md) retains LegacyReports consuming its fixture TaskBoard through public HTTP, without importing private classes or opening its database. It verifies public compatibility, startup dependency expansion and cleanup. Its old generated IDs/trimmed titles/idempotent completion belong only to that fixture; the canonical examples follow Atlas.

Profile cases remain callable independently of HTTP/CLI. Boundaries resolve trusted context and project results; cases authorize and own their algorithms. Interaction choices are identified as L4. Apps integrate through documented public contracts; transport and database shortcuts do not substitute for them.
