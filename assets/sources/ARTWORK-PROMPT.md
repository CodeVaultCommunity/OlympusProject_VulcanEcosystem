## Exact generation prompt

Ultra-wide banner composition about 3:1, target1600x534. Deep midnight navy #061525 with electric blue/cyan/teal light and restrained warm gold sparks, elegant premium scientific editorial realism. The LEFT 47 percent must stay very dark and calm with only subtle atmosphere, completely empty of objects so editorial text and an existing logo can later be overlaid. Concentrate all meaningful artwork on the RIGHT 53 percent, full scene safely inside right area: a sculptural futuristic dark metallic anvil on a refined digital forge platform, a hammer hovering at a slight angle above a glowing modular crystalline assembly on the anvil, delicate illuminated circuit traces and several translucent geometric software module blocks being fitted together, tiny precise golden sparks frozen at the join. Evoke implementation, composition of applications, crafted executable systems and Vulcan's forge, with refined Greek-inspired material craftsmanship, but no figures or gods. Very faint volumetric navy atmosphere and celestial dust match an elegant scientific atlas banner. Clean silhouette, beautiful metal and glass, rich detail, controlled contrast. The overall canvas should stay navy, no large white areas, no smoky industrial grunge. No logos, no text, no letters, no readable numbers, no title, no watermark, no pseudo code, no terminal or screenshots. Image is decorative only, not a technical diagram.
